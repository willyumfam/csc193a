Space Exploration: Humanity's Greatest Adventure

Space exploration is one of the most inspiring and transformative endeavors in human history. From the first moon landing in 1969 to the ongoing missions to Mars, our curiosity about the universe continues to push the boundaries of science and technology. Organizations like NASA are working every day to expand our understanding of the cosmos, develop new spacecraft, and plan for future crewed missions to Mars. Space exploration has also given us countless everyday technologies — including GPS, memory foam, and water filtration systems.
